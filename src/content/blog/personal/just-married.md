It has been a moving and wonderful few weeks. On November 1st, I married the love of my life in a beautiful Orthodox ceremony at our home parish in San Antonio. It was at once terrifying and touching to walk into the church and see everyone in my life there, waiting to watch us be brought together. I plan to caption a recording of the ceremony to make it easier to share, but I haven't decided yet whether I'll post it here.

It was my first time wearing makeup in maybe seven years, but my sisters did an incredible job glamming me up. My bridesmaids were held up getting to the church because one of their dogs escaped the house right as I was leaving, but it all turned out fine! The reception was incredibly fun, with impromptu karaoke and just generally enjoying the company of my family and friends. What I wasn't expecting was for the wine to be sold by the bottle? I was told it would be by the glass, but that just didn't happen. Oh well, something always has to go wrong at a wedding somehow, and that's not the worst thing in the world.

## The honeymoon

By the end of the night we were both exhausted, though not as much as I thought we'd be, and we got a solid two hours of sleep before heading to the airport to catch a flight to the [Poconos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocono_Mountains) in Pennsylvania. It used to be a tourist hot spot, but these days it's quite sleepy and uneventful, which was exactly what we wanted. Amazingly, the trees were still a gorgeous shade of orange, and there were plenty of interesting restaurants and coffee shops to try. One of them was a [Bitcoin-themed café](https://erickimphotography.com/bitcoin-and-coffee-a-comprehensive-exploration/), which I had never heard of before, but apparently they exist in a lot of places. We hit up a couple of antique shops, a weird [candle shop](https://mycandleshoppe.com/), and an amazing [wood-fired pizza place](https://www.poconobrewery.com/pizza), and eventually drove into Philadelphia to visit the [Philadelphia Museum of Art](https://www.philamuseum.org/) and try a Philly cheesesteak at [Reading Terminal Market](https://readingterminalmarket.org/). Philly was incredibly nasty, sorry, but the market was phenomenal.

![Our hands resting together, showing our wedding rings, over a bed of autumn leaves](/images/wedding_rings.webp)

Toward the end of the trip, we went on a [train tour with a wine and cheese tasting](https://www.newhoperailroad.com/grapevineexpress/), which was great and very cozy, except that it was pitch black outside, so the "tour of the area" part didn't really work.

## Back home

We flew back late Saturday night and have been enjoying our time off since. I've been trying new bread recipes, and they came out surprisingly well! I love baking but never have time for it, so this has been a good chance to finally do some.

![A braided loaf of bread covered in sesame seeds](/images/sesame_braided_bread.webp)

Last night we sat around our firepit roasting hot dogs and sipping our favorite tequila. Seriously, how do you craft a tequila this good? It goes down like nothing, and I never get drunk or hungover from it. José Cuervo, if you're reading this, I love you.
