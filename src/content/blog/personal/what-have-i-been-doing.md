When I say this year has been big for me, I'm not exaggerating at all. I feel like a dog with its head out the window going 180 MPH, mouth dry, eyes watering, barely handling it, but also not wanting to be anywhere else.

I graduated with my BBA in Cybersecurity from UTSA on August 4th. Four years of blood sweat and tears but now I have a degree! Whether it will serve me well in the current job market, uh, we'll see. I've been applying for jobs every day for the past 6 months to a year in varying levels of urgency, and there hasn't been a single bite except for one interview with no reply since I finished it.

I'm not going to fall into the depressing and, frankly, cringey doomer attitude you see on tech subreddits, though. I just don't see the use of scrolling through post after post on "Should I quit tech and become a plumber??" or "No one is hiring me!!!" much less make one myself. I have projects to work on, skills to hone, and I love technology more than anything. I'm addicted to this life, to cybersecurity, to tinkering, and I'm not looking for a cure; I'm looking for someone to hire me for it. 😅

On that note, here's what I've been working on:

- Learning AWS
- Learning Windows Active Directory
- Programming in Python more
- Strengthening my homelab (always)
  - Specifically health monitoring and foolproof backup plans, as well as setting up a proper VLAN
  - I also need to properly add my second hard drive to the ZFS pool
- Sending the physical wedding invitations (my wedding is on November 1st, if you were curious. And yes, I am planning it pretty much by myself, but it's almost all done)
- Making a cybersecurity-focused website with a newsletter. Currently a MVP
- Reading as much as possible!
- Growing spiritually. I was baptized August 5th on the Transfiguration, so I have been focusing a lot on prayer and attending services since then[^1]
- Thinking of more features or changes for this site
- Setting up a locally run LLM on my MacBook
- Trying, and struggling, to journal more again
- Building a terminal-only machine. I want to make a post dedicated to this project, but basically, a focus on no GUI and only terminal tools for everything. This is both to get even more used to the terminal and for fun!

If this seems like too much to "currently" be working on, it's because it is. I am driving myself to utter insanity, but it's pretty fun.

Some other things going on are regular hang-outs with my friends, including a Tiki Party me and my fiancé are hosting at our house this weekend. We also just came back from Fredericksburg today, which was extremely fun and exhausting; we walked through fine art galleries, ate german food, and attended the Blessing of the Grapes at a local winery done by the Fredericksburg Orthodox Church. Then, I have a beach trip coming up with some friends soon!

Hope to be in touch again soon and post more. There are some changes I made to the site, mainly information updates, and more to come that you may notice. Have a wonderful week.
